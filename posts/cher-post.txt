Cher left Sonny Bono in 1975 with $5,000 a month. The company with her name on it, she says, was 95% his and 5% his lawyer's.

She says she could not sign a check without Sonny's or his lawyer's signature. Her contract gave her three weeks of vacation a year and a salary she was "likely never paid."

In the video below, Howard Stern asks her about the money. She tells what she said to Sonny: "I was there with you every day for 12 years."

She says they once borrowed money from their own chauffeur, because they had nothing.

When they divorced, she says Sonny offered her whatever she wanted. She took her own place in Malibu and $5,000 a month.

It was David Geffen who read her contract and told her who owned what.

She says she asked Sonny why. His answer, in her telling: "I knew you'd always leave me."

She told him that was not good enough. In 2024, 26 years after he died, she told The New York Times: "He took all my money."

The case for Cher. She told Sonny she made half of the money.

The case for Sonny. He built the business, and on paper she was his employee.

Do the math. 12 years of hits, and she walked away with a house and $5,000 a month.
