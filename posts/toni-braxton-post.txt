Toni Braxton's records brought in about $170 million. From her first contract, she says, she got a check for $1,972.

In the video below, ABC News asks her if that's true. "Yeah, that's right. That's true."

Her debut album came out in 1993. "Un-Break My Heart" spent 11 weeks at No. 1. By 1997 she had 5 Grammys.

The catch was in the contract. "What happens is they'll give you an advancement on the next record and then the next record," she said. Every advance is paid back out of her own royalties.

In 1997 she sued her label to get out of the deal. In 1998 she filed for Chapter 7 bankruptcy.

She admits she spent, too. "I love dishes and house things. So I kind of lost it a little bit." Thousand-thread-count sheets, she added.

In 1999 she settled with the label and signed a new contract reported at $20 million.

Then she got sick. Her Las Vegas show ended, and in 2010 she filed for bankruptcy again, with reported debts of up to $50 million.

"Because you're supposed to be smarter," she said.

Her records made $170 million. Her check said $1,972.

Who failed Toni Braxton more: the record label or Toni herself?
