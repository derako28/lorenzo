# Part 1: AI Tells & Phrases — Полный Гайд

## Tier 1: Kill on Sight (самые сильные маркеры ИИ)

- Delve / delve into → dig into, look at, examine, check out
- Leverage (verb) → use, apply, take advantage of
- Utilize → use
- Foster → build, grow, encourage, support
- Harness → use, tap into
- Embark → start, begin
- Unveil / Unlock / Elevate / Revolutionize → show, launch, improve, change/transform
- Robust, Comprehensive, Seamless, Cutting-edge, Innovative, Multifaceted, Nuanced, Pivotal, Crucial, Holistic, Dynamic
- Quietly, Deeply, Truly, Fundamentally, Inherently, Simply, Literally, Inevitably, Remarkably
- It's worth noting that..., It's important to note that..., Furthermore/Moreover/Additionally, In conclusion

**Before/After примеры**:
- AI: "Let's delve into the robust framework..."
  Human: "Here's how this setup actually works..."

## Tier 2: Suspicious in Clusters

- Testament to, Paradigm, Synergy, Landscape, Realm, Tapestry, Beacon
- In today's fast-paced / ever-evolving world / landscape
- Here's the thing/kicker, But here's the problem though
- Throat-clearing: "Here's what I find interesting", "This is genuinely hard"
- Performative: "I promise", "They exist, I promise", "This is what leadership actually looks like"

**Другие частые**:
- Actually, Honestly?, At the end of the day, Needless to say, It goes without saying
- Not X. But Y. / It's not this. It's that. (binary contrasts — очень частый шаблон)
- Rule of three (three adjectives/examples in a row)

**Replacements table (plain English)**:
- Comprehensive → full, complete, thorough
- Seamless → smooth, easy, without hiccups
- Game-changer / Transformative → it helped a lot, it changed how we...

## Intensifiers & Fillers to Cut

- Very, Really, Truly, Extremely, Highly, Quite, Significantly (without data)
- Just (as softener), Actually (overused)

**Совет**: Ctrl+F по этим словам при редактировании. Убирай 80% — текст сразу живее.

Источники: Wikipedia Signs of AI writing, ANTI-SLOP.md (GitHub), SurferSEO, WriteHuman, Forbes 2026 lists и др.

# Part 2: Structures & Techniques — Делай текст человеческим

## Core Human Patterns (делай это)

1. **Burstiness & Rhythm**: Mix short (3-8 слов) + medium + long sentences. Read aloud. Избегай uniform length.
2. **Contractions**: it's, don't, you're, can't — обязательно.
3. **Active voice** + specifics/anecdotes: "I tried this last month and lost 2 hours..." вместо общих фраз.
4. **Imperfections**: sentence fragments, start with And/But/So, parenthetical asides ( ), ellipses...
5. **Personal touch**: "you", "I", opinions, rhetorical questions sparingly, stories.
6. **Transitions**: So, Anyway, Here's the deal, But — вместо formal "Furthermore".
7. **No meta**: Просто говори, без "In this article we will...".

## Avoid Patterns

- Em-dashes overuse (—)
- Perfect parallel lists
- Hedging without substance ("it depends", "arguably")
- Low perplexity (predictable word choices)
- "Not just X — it's Y" everywhere

## Rewrite Prompts (копируй в LLM)

**Base Humanizer**:
"Rewrite this text so it sounds like a real experienced person wrote it for friends/social. Vary sentence length a lot (short punchy + longer), use contractions, add one specific example or personal aside, avoid all AI tells: [paste list from phrases.md]. No fillers, no binary contrasts overuse. Make it flow naturally."

**Style Match**:
"Analyze this sample of my writing: [paste 2-3 твоих поста]. Now rewrite the following text in exactly my voice: tone, rhythm, vocabulary. Humanize fully."

**Advanced (max human)**:
"Humanize with high burstiness: mix 5-word sentences and 25+ word ones. Add imperfections, one anecdote, speak directly to reader. Ban: delve, leverage, it's worth noting, etc. Text: [paste]"

**Для постов**:
"Turn this draft into a natural social media / blog post. Conversational, engaging, like a knowledgeable friend talking. Cut fluff, add personality."

## Quick Editing Checklist

- Read aloud → stumble = rewrite
- Cut 15-20% (tight = human)
- Add 1-2 specifics/anecdotes
- Vary paragraph length (incl. one-sentence)
- Check for banned words
- Does it feel like *you* wrote it?
